# Smartphone Comparison — Expert Reviewer Framework

- **Category:** Technology & Product Comparisons
- **Provenance:** User-authored short instruction dated 9 November 2026; expanded into a reusable template
- **Description:** Compare a shortlist of smartphones from the perspective of an experienced reviewer and real-world user, then rank the options.
- **Purpose:** Make a purchase decision based on meaningful differences rather than spec-sheet repetition.
- **Tags:** `smartphones`, `buying-guide`, `product-review`, `comparison`, `ranking`

## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Act as an experienced smartphone reviewer and practical everyday smartphone user.

Compare the following phones and help me decide which one is the best purchase:

{{PHONE_1}}
{{PHONE_2}}
{{PHONE_3}}
{{PHONE_4}}

## Review criteria
Evaluate each phone across:
- Real-world performance and sustained performance.
- Display quality and outdoor visibility.
- Main, ultrawide, telephoto, and selfie camera quality where applicable.
- Battery life and charging speed.
- Software experience, update policy, and long-term reliability.
- Build quality, ergonomics, and durability.
- Connectivity and useful features.
- Service availability and ownership experience in {{COUNTRY_OR_MARKET}}.
- Price and value for money at the current available prices.

## Analysis instructions
- Verify current specifications, software support, and prices using reliable sources.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from subjective reviewer judgement.
- Do not assume that a higher specification automatically means a better real-world experience.
- Explain meaningful trade-offs and identify who each phone is best suited for.
- Consider the actual prices: {{BUDGET_OR_PRICE_CONTEXT}}.
- If a detail is unavailable or disputed, say so instead of guessing.

## Final output
1. A concise comparison table.
2. Strengths and weaknesses of each phone.
3. The most important differences that affect everyday use.
4. Best phone for different user priorities.
5. A final ranking from best to least suitable.
6. One clear overall recommendation with reasons.
```

## Customization

Replace phone names, market, budget, and any personal priorities (camera, gaming, battery, compact size, software, or longevity). For a fair comparison, provide the exact storage/RAM variants and prices if known.

## Source notes

The user explicitly requested an experienced phone reviewer/user perspective, differentiation between Samsung S25, Pixel 10a, OnePlus Nord 6, and iQOO 15R, and rankings at the end. The reusable framework generalizes that instruction without assuming the same shortlist in future comparisons.
