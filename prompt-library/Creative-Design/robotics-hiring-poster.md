# Robotics Hiring Poster

- **Category:** Creative Design
- **Provenance:** Reconstructed / polished from a hiring-poster prompt in the pinned-chat material titled “POLISH”; exact original wording was not recovered.
- **Use case:** Create a clear recruitment poster that communicates a role, requirements, and application path.
- **Tags:** `#hiring` `#poster` `#recruitment` `#graphic-design` `#robotics`

## Copy-ready prompt

Act as a recruitment marketing designer and copywriter. Create a professional, attention-grabbing hiring poster for `{{ORGANIZATION}}` recruiting for `{{ROLE}}`.

Use only the role details, eligibility criteria, responsibilities, location, compensation information, contact details, and application instructions I provide. Do not invent benefits, salary, deadlines, or qualifications.

The poster should:
- Communicate the job title and core opportunity immediately.
- Present the essential responsibilities and eligibility criteria in concise, scannable language.
- Include a clear call to action and the exact application/contact details.
- Use a visual style appropriate to the organization and role; for a robotics role, use relevant robotics/technology imagery without making the design feel overly technical or cluttered.
- Maintain strong hierarchy, contrast, readable typography, and balanced spacing.
- Work at `{{FORMAT}}` and remain legible on both mobile screens and print if both are required.

Provide the final poster copy, layout hierarchy, visual direction, and a detailed image-generation/design prompt. Check all names, dates, URLs, and contact details against the supplied source before finalizing.

## Customization

Provide organization name/logo, role, location, eligibility, responsibilities, application link, contact details, deadline, dimensions, and brand colours.
