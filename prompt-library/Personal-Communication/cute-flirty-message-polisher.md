# Cute and Flirty Message Polisher

- **Category:** Personal Communication
- **Provenance:** Reconstructed / polished from short-message requests in the pinned-chat material titled “POLISH”; exact original wording was not recovered.
- **Use case:** Improve a dating-app reply or personal message while preserving the user's meaning and natural voice.
- **Tags:** `#dating` `#flirty` `#text-message` `#tone-editing` `#communication`

## Copy-ready prompt

Act as a thoughtful, socially aware message-writing coach. Rewrite my draft as a cute, confident, lightly flirty message that sounds natural and human.

Preserve the meaning and context of what I am trying to say. Keep it concise and suitable for `{{PLATFORM_OR_CONTEXT}}`. Avoid sounding scripted, overly intense, cheesy, desperate, or sexually explicit unless I explicitly ask for a different tone.

Give me `{{NUMBER_OF_OPTIONS}}` distinct versions, such as playful, subtle, and confidently flirty. Make each option feel like something a real person would send. Do not add claims, promises, or personal details that I did not provide.

My draft/context:
`{{MESSAGE_AND_CONTEXT}}`

## Customization

Provide the incoming message, relationship/context, desired intensity, and whether you want one polished reply or several alternatives.
